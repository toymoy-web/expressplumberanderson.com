About The Neighborhood

West Central Historic District, Anderson

West Central is a small residential district just west of downtown Anderson. The National Register of Historic Places describes it as roughly bounded by Brown-Delaware, 10th, John and 13th Streets, an area of about 30 acres. It was listed on the National Register in December 1984, and the City of Anderson designated it a local historic district in March 1985.

The district is mostly residential, with a few commercial buildings. Its houses date from the late 1800s into the first half of the twentieth century, and the city names Italianate, Bungalow, Dutch Colonial, American Foursquare and Shingle styles among them. Lincoln Park sits on Lincoln Street between 12th and 13th Streets.

The neighborhood grew during the 1880s gas boom, according to Indiana Landmarks. After the wells ran dry and the Depression arrived, many of the large houses were divided into smaller units. The Anderson Downtown Neighbors Association, formed in 1980, works to improve and preserve properties here.

What That Means For Plumbing

Look Before You Assume

We do not guess what is behind a wall from a neighborhood's age. A house that has been remodeled more than once can hold original plumbing, later additions and repairs from several decades side by side. The only reliable way to know is to check what is actually installed.

Houses that were split into several units can also have drain and supply lines that were rerouted to serve each one. If you manage or live in a converted house, tell us how the layout changed. That helps us trace a problem to the right fixture faster.

Sewer Line Condition

A camera inspection shows the real condition of your lateral, so you can decide on repairs from what is on screen instead of a guess.

Supply Line Material

A quick look at visible pipe in the basement or crawl space tells us what you have and whether it needs attention.

Water Heater Age

Check the date on the tank's label. Units near the end of their typical life are worth planning for before they fail.

Hidden Leaks

Unexplained water bills or damp spots near old walls call for leak detection before any wall is opened.
