Repair, Not Full Replacement

When A Spot Repair Is Enough

Sewer line repair covers a localized problem, a cracked section, a root intrusion point, or an offset joint, rather than a line that has failed along its full length. A camera inspection identifies exactly where the damage is before we recommend anything, since guessing at a location means digging in the wrong spot or lining a section that didn't need it.

Anderson's older neighborhoods near downtown and the White River corridor still have original clay lateral lines in some cases, and clay pipe joints are the most common point where tree roots work their way in over years, eventually cracking or displacing the joint.

Repair Options

Spot Repair Vs. Trenchless Lining

A localized crack or offset joint is often fixed with a direct spot repair, digging down to that section and replacing it. A line with multiple problem points along its length, or root intrusion at several joints, is often a better fit for trenchless pipe lining, which installs a cured-in-place resin liner meeting ASTM F1216, the standard governing cured-in-place pipe rehabilitation, through existing access points without trenching the entire yard.

The right choice depends on how many problem points the camera finds and how accessible the line is, which we walk through with the actual footage before quoting either option.

What To Expect

Timeline For A Typical Repair

A single spot repair is usually completed in one day once the camera inspection has confirmed the location, including backfilling the excavation. Trenchless lining typically takes a full day as well, though the resin lining needs several hours to cure before the line can be used again.

Either way, we test the repaired section with a follow-up camera pass before considering the job finished, not just a water-flow test that could miss a lingering problem further down the line.

Landscaping restoration after a spot repair is usually limited to the excavation area itself, which we backfill and grade level, though re-seeding or sod is typically a separate line item from the plumbing repair.
