The Maintenance Step Tank Units Don't Need

Why Tankless Units Need A Real Descale Schedule

A tankless water heater heats water as it passes through a narrow heat exchanger, which means mineral scale from hard water builds up on a much smaller surface area than in a traditional tank. On Anderson's groundwater-sourced municipal supply, skipping the annual flush is one of the most common reasons a tankless unit throws an error code or loses capacity years before it should.

A flush circulates a descaling solution through the unit to dissolve that buildup, which is a service most manufacturers specify annually to keep the warranty valid, not an optional upsell.

Service

What A Flush And Inspection Involves

A proper descale service isolates the unit with dedicated valves, circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger for roughly 45 minutes to an hour, then flushes it clean and checks the inlet filter and venting. For a new install, we also confirm gas line sizing and venting length meet the manufacturer's spec, since an undersized gas line is a common cause of a unit that can't hit its rated output.

Gas & Electric Requirements

Why Some Homes Need Upgrades Before Install

Tankless units draw significantly more gas volume in a short burst than a tank unit does, since they heat water on demand rather than maintaining a stored reserve. A gas line sized correctly for a 40-gallon tank is often undersized for a tankless replacement under the Indiana Fuel Gas Code standard (675 IAC 25) sizing tables. That shows up as a unit that can't hit its rated temperature rise during high-demand use like a shower running with the dishwasher going.

We size the gas line to the specific unit's BTU rating before installation, not after, since running a new gas line after the fact costs more than sizing it correctly the first time.

Electric tankless units avoid the gas-sizing question entirely but often need a dedicated high-amperage circuit, and some older Anderson homes with a 100-amp panel need an electrical service upgrade before an electric tankless unit is even an option worth pricing out.
