Diagnosis First

Why We Diagnose Before We Quote

A water heater that stopped producing hot water can mean a failed heating element, a tripped high-limit switch, a bad thermocouple on a gas unit, or a tank that has simply reached the end of its service life. Those are four different repairs at four different price points, so a technician checks the unit's age, the pattern of the failure, and whether there's active water at the base of the tank before recommending anything.

If the tank itself is leaking at a seam or fitting rather than a loose connection, that is usually the point where repair stops being the honest recommendation, and we will tell you that directly rather than patching a tank that is going to fail again within months.

Commonly a failing thermocouple on gas units, a straightforward same-visit fix.

Diagnosis

How We Find The Actual Cause

Every water heater repair call starts with checking the data plate for unit age, testing the thermostat and heating element with a multimeter on electric units, and checking gas pressure and thermocouple voltage on gas units. We also check for sediment buildup at the drain valve, which is a common cause of rumbling noise and reduced capacity on Anderson's harder water supply.

If the unit is under 6 years old and the tank itself is not leaking, repair is almost always the right call. Past that age, we walk you through the honest repair-versus-replace math rather than defaulting to whichever costs more.

Warranty Considerations

Repair Vs. Warranty Claim

Most tank water heaters carry a manufacturer warranty on the tank itself for 6 to 12 years, separate from any labor warranty on the original installation. It's separate again from the requirements of the Indiana Plumbing Code standard (675 IAC 16) that a replacement install has to meet regardless of which brand or warranty term you choose. If your unit is still within that tank warranty window, the manufacturer may cover a replacement tank at no cost, though labor to install it is usually a separate charge unless the original installer's labor warranty is also still active.

We check the data plate and serial number against the manufacturer's warranty lookup before quoting a repair or replacement, since that changes the honest math on whether a repair, a warranty claim, or an out-of-pocket replacement is actually your cheapest path forward.
